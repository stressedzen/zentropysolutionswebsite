ABOUT ME

Null.Kitten is a visual artist and event technician working across lights, screens, sound, and strange little systems. The project grew out of performance spaces — VJ work, lighting, live tech, and the soft chaos behind a room coming alive.

The music is part of the same machine: self-written, self-built, and tuned for late rooms, damaged signals, and people who like their technology with fingerprints on it.

VISUALS / VJ

Live visuals built in real time — feeds, loops, and generated imagery pushed to screens and walls. The set reacts to the room: whatever the music does, the picture answers.

EVENT TECH

The practical side of making a night happen — patching, rigging, signal flow, and the quiet problem-solving that keeps a show running. Comfortable in the dark behind the desk.

LIGHTING

Lighting design and operation for performances and rooms. Programming fixtures, shaping mood, and reading a crowd through what they can and can’t quite see.

    ROOM TONE

    Loaded in before doors, patched the rig, and let the room sit empty for a while. There’s a sound a space makes before anyone arrives — the hum of the building, the lights settling. I like to tune to that first.

    By the second set the screens were answering the bass on their own. Soft chaos, the good kind.
