What Zentropy does

A practical design and manufacturing house.

We help turn ideas, broken parts, scans, sketches, and half-formed concepts into physical things that can be tested, improved, and used.

The work sits between design studio, engineering bench, and manufacturing floor: enough creativity to shape the idea, enough technical discipline to make it real, and enough production sense to keep the next step grounded.

How we work

Make the first version real, then make it better.

We keep the process direct: understand the part, choose the right path, build a useful first version, then refine around fit, finish, strength, and repeatability.

  1. Brief

    Define the goal, constraints, dimensions, finish, use case, and success criteria.

  2. Model

    Create or clean up CAD from sketches, scans, reference parts, or existing files.

  3. Make

    Print, scan, test, and prepare the part with the right material and process.

  4. Refine

    Improve the design around real-world feedback before repeating or scaling it.

Project fit

Bring the messy middle.

  • A part exists, but the digital file does not.
  • A concept needs CAD before it can become physical.
  • A prototype needs to be printed, tested, and changed quickly.
  • A detailed model needs the finish and precision of resin printing.
  • A custom part needs design thinking and manufacturing sense together.
